How this figure is generated

These heatmaps measure disagreement among FOMC participants using the cross-sectional interquartile range (IQR) of individual federal funds rate projections. Within each SEP release and horizon, the IQR is the 75th percentile minus the 25th percentile. Higher values indicate greater dispersion in participants’ views.

For an SEP released in year t, the 1-year-ahead horizon corresponds to t+1, the 2-year-ahead horizon to t+2, and the longer-run panel uses the SEP longer-run projections directly. All panels use the same color scale, allowing direct comparisons across horizons and over time. The IQR measures disagreement, not uncertainty.
